A walkthrough of the various steps required to complete mammogram appointments.

## Opening a clinic session

A clinic session is a group of breast screening appointments at a specified location at a particular time.

The 'Clinics' option in the main menu takes you to any session taking place today that you have access to. These will be across the units or mobile vans where you work.

You can also see any upcoming or recently completed clinics.

Select a clinic to see details of all the scheduled appointments organised by time. You can refine this list using the:

- search tool and checkboxes (accessed by clicking 'Filter appointments')
- status tabs (checked in, in progress, completed, etc)

The list can be sorted by any column that contains a time. This allows you to see which participants checked in first or which appointments were most recently completed.

## Checking in participants

Each participant needs to be checked in before their appointment can begin.

Click the 'Check in' link for the relevant appointment to confirm their identity using the information provided.

If there is any doubt over their identity, the appointment can be stopped at this point.

## Starting and running an appointment

A 'Start appointment' action is available for all participants who have been checked in.

The mammographer who is taking the participant through their appointment should click this button once they are in the mammogram room. This will begin a four-stage workflow.

### 1. Confirm identity

A second identity check is required by the mammographer running an appointment once they are in the mammogram room with the participant.

This is also an opportunity to add or confirm the participant's ethnicity.

Click the 'Confirm identity' button to proceed.

### 2. Review medical information

Various forms are available to capture any information provided by the participant during the appointment.

They are organised into:

- **Mammogram history** - see the most recent mammogram and add any that are not on the system
- **Medical history** - such as breast cancer, implants, prior procedures and treatments
- **Symptoms and signs** - including lumps, swelling, rashes or nipple changes
- **Breast features** - such as non-surgical scars or moles
- **HRT, pregnancy and breast feeding** - to capture information that may affect breast density

Some forms require extra steps (such as requiring consent if the participant has breast implants) or suggest you end the appointment (for example if they mention a mammogram within the last 6 months).

All medical history sections are optional. If there is nothing to add you can choose to 'Complete all and authorise imaging'.

  <div class="nhsuk-details__text">
    <p>To comply with IR(ME)R (the Ionising Radiation (Medical Exposure) Regulations) each appointment needs to have someone to justify the required mammogram images, adopting the role of 'Practitioner' from the regulations.</p>
    <p>The decision to proceed can be made at this stage in the appointment after:</p>
    <ul>
    <li>confirming the participant's identity</li>
    <li>seeing the reason for screening - a screening protocol (such as 'Routine') is shown in the information bar throughout the appointment</li>
    <li>understanding their relevant medical history</li>
    </ul>
  </div>
</details>

If the process at your clinic is to hand the participant over to another mammographer to take the images, you can choose to change the operator and then 'Exit appointment' so it's ready for them to resume.

### 3. Take mammogram images

Each appointment should automatically be on the worklist of mammogram machines at the clinic location.

If this is the case, you will see 'On worklist' with a green tick in the appointment information bar.

When you start taking images, thumbnails of each view will be displayed on Rubie after a few seconds. If they all appear as expected, click 'Confirm all images received' to continue.

If you have taken more than a standard set of images (2 CC and 2 MLO), the next step will be to say if these were additional images or repeats, and why they were required.

### 4. Check information

The final step before completing is confirming that the information collected throughout the appointment is correct.

This provides an opportunity to check and change any details, as well as adding an appointment note if there's any necessary information to share with image readers.

Once you're satisfied, click 'Complete screening and return to clinic'. This will make the participant's images available for reading.

## Closing a clinic session

On each clinic screen, a 'Close clinic' link is available which takes you to a summary of the status of all appointments from that clinic.

If all appointments have a 'completed' status (such as Screened, Attended not screened, or Cancelled) you can 'Confirm and close clinic'.

If there are any outstanding appointments (participants either in progress, checked in but not started, or not checked in) then you need to take the necessary actions to complete these appointments before the clinic can be marked as closed.
