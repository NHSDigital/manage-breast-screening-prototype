{% set commandsHtml %}
  • Connect and join: opens the socket and sends JoinContext. The reply carries a GUID that every later message must quote.
  • Show: ShowStudy by accession number. Show with patient ID adds the patient ID and its issuer. Show with last presentation asks for the viewer's last hanging protocol or presentation state.
  • Close: CloseStudy. Each show creates a viewer session and the viewer allows four, so close before showing the next. The cycle button does this for you.
  • Heartbeat: sent automatically at the interval on the left, and on demand. The viewer rejects one whose timestamp is more than 10 seconds old.
  • Leave and disconnect: LeaveContext, then closes the socket.
  • Anything the viewer sends on its own (a study opened from its worklist, switching to idle, logout) is logged too.
